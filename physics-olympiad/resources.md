# Resources, Checkpoints, and Full Bibliography

This file holds everything that isn't topic-specific: the media diet (YouTube/podcasts/courses), the past-exam archives, the checkpoint/mock-exam calendar, and a complete, exact bibliography of every source named across all eight topic files. Every entry below is a full citation — author(s), exact title, edition, publisher, year (plus journal/volume/pages for papers) — so nothing here should require guessing at an edition or searching for a title fragment.

## Checkpoint and mock-exam calendar (topic-completion-based, not calendar-based)

| After you finish... | Checkpoint |
|---|---|
| `00-mathematical-toolkit.md` + first pass of `01-mechanics.md` through Newton's laws | Diagnostic: 20 mixed algebra/trig/vector/kinematics problems, 90 minutes, zero look-ups |
| `01-mechanics.md` in full (Tier 1) | F=ma-level diagnostic, full length (AAPT archive) |
| `01-mechanics.md` + `03-oscillations-and-waves.md` | Timed 3-hour mixed mechanics + oscillations problem set |
| `02-electromagnetism.md` in full | Full USAPhO-difficulty mock, mechanics + E&M (AAPT archive) |
| `05-thermodynamics-and-statistical-physics.md` in full | Full mock, mechanics + E&M + thermodynamics |
| `06-modern-physics-relativity-and-quantum.md` in full | First full mock IPhO theory paper(s), 5 hours, drawn cold from the ipho-unofficial.org archive |
| `04-optics.md` + one full pass through `07-experimental-physics.md` | Second full mock IPhO + first full mock experimental exam (5 hours) |
| All seven content topics complete, Tier 1 everywhere | Two more full mock IPhO theory papers plus two more mock experimental exams, spaced a week apart, each followed by a full error-log review |
| Final phase, whenever you judge yourself ready | Final full mock IPhO (theory + experimental), real exam-day conditions, followed by a genuine taper before any real exam date |

**Terminal milestone:** cold-solve a full, previously unseen past IPhO paper (theory + experiment) within the official time limits and score it yourself against the official marking scheme.
